Getting Started

  1. Connect your PromoBeacon device to your computer via USB
  2. Click "Connect Device" and select your ESP32 from the list
  3. Firmware files will be downloaded automatically from the server
  4. Click "Flash Firmware" to begin the flashing process
  5. Wait for the process to complete - the device will restart automatically
